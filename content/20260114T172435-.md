# Information Geometry
Parameters live on a manifold

## Example
Covariance Matrix of a [2D Gaussian Distribution](20260120T180416-multivariate_gaussian_distribution.md)

$$
\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix}
\mathrm{var}(X) & \mathrm{cov}(X, Y)\\
\mathrm{cov}(X, Y) & \mathrm{Var}(Y)
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Renaming the variables to something simple
$$
\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\\\
b & c
\end{bmatrix}
$$


Notice the constraints on this matrix
- We know that $a, c$ are positive since they are variances
