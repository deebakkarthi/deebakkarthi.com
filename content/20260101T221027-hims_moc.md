# HIMS
- Health Information Management System (we choose HIMS first and decided to expand the acronym using vaguely related terms) is our attempt of an Apple watch clone or a very small part of its function - Heart rate and  Blood oxygen level
- We also performed rudimentary activity recognition

# Hardware
- We used a [ESP32](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/socs/esp32) which has wifi and bluetooth support
- We used MAX30100 pulse-oximetry IC
- MPU 6050 as our gyroscope
