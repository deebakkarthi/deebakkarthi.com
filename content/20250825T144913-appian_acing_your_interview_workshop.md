# What is Appian

- Could often see them on grounds during fairs

# Type of interviews
## Online interviews
- These are not technically interviews
- This is the recruiter looking at your linkedin or handshake profile
- Rupamatch is another site
- Since the profile pages are not limited like a one page resume put as much stuff as possible
- The recruiter are just gonna scroll. They won't get tired scrolling. So put as many keywords as possible. 
- Many companies have like a crawler that searches for these keywords. This is how some send out recruiting emails

## Career fair interviews
- These too are not technically interviews. These are the interactions you have with recruiters in fairs and other career events
- When talking to someone please have a clear objective about what you want to get out of the interaction
- Dont ask stuff that can be found easily by googling

> How you made them feel is the most important 

- Lead with a joke or be passionate and personable
- Prepare different pitches for different companies

## Actual interviews
- Know the expectation of the interviewer
- During a Phone screen interview the recruiter just wants to know your interests and stuff. They are not highly technical in nature
- Video interview with the team or a hiring manager is going to be technical

# Strategies
## Coordination
- Be flexible
- Let them know when you will be free upfront
## Preparation
- Stalk the interviewer on Linkedin
- no, they won't mind you if it shows "X viewed your profile"
- Recruiters usually deal with high level stuff while team/hiring manager can be low level and technical
- Questions are a two-way street so ask questions yourself
## Practice
- Practice a lot
- Practice "Tell me about yourself" a million times
	- Use the STAR method
		- Situation
		- Task
		- Action
		- Result
	- Write down all of the results that you have achieved and work in reverse
## Selling and Closing
- What did you learn and how will that help in this role
- Have a good answer for "why " you want this job
	- Have something extremely personal instead of platitudes
