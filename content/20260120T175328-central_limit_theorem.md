# Layman's version
- Suppose we have some(any) distribution. 
- We take a sample of size $n$ and compute its *mean* and call it $\bar{x}$
- We repeat this process several times
- The distribution of $\bar{x}$ converges to a [normal distribution](20260120T172840-gaussian_distribution.md) as $n \rightarrow \infty$
