![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020260904170300.png)

These are not **inherited**. It is actually the other way around.

All Inner products can induce a valid norm
All norms can induce a valid metric
All metrics can induce a valid topology

It grows from the inside. Though it was introduced as *adding length to X gives you Y*, I think it is actually *Y having length also makes it a X*.
