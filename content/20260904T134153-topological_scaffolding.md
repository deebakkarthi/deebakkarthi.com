As seen in [Geometry of Data](20260101T183023-geometry_of_data_moc.md), we can construct a scaffolding to explain mathematics
1. [Set](20260904T101310-set_moc.md)
	1. These are the base of mathematics
2. [Space](20260904T101354-space_moc.md)
	1. Sets with some *structure*
3. [Topology](20260904T104310-topological_space_moc.md)
	1. Space by virtue of defining *open-sets*
4. [Metric](20260904T101420-metric_space_moc.md)
	1. A subset of Topology where a function called metric is defined and gives a metaphorical *distance*
	2. All metric spaces induce a valid topology
	3. All topologies don't automatically have a valid metric
	4. We only care about topologies with a metric
5. Hausdorff Space
	1. A subset of topologies with disjoint neighborhoods
6. Manifolds
	1. Hausdorff spaces which are locally euclidean
7. Differentiable Manifold
	1. A manifold with an *atlas* that defines *smoothness*
8.  Riemannian Manifold
	1. Differentiable manifold with a Reimannian metric defined
