SR Latch stands for *Set-Reset Latch*. It is a 1-bit memory component
![](../assets/Pasted%20image%2020251231142220.png)

- It has two NOR gates that are linked up as above
- If `S` goes high, `Q` is also high and stay high until `R` goes high
- If `R` goes high then `Q` goes low and stays low until `S` goes high
- $\bar{Q}$ is always the opposite of $Q$
