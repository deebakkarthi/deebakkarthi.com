- What the paper is about, What is the author's viewpoint, why do they think this is important, their work
- What is the *useful* contribution of this paper. Most papers make contributions but not all of them are useful or correct. If it is not abundantly clear what they have contributed let them know. 
- Maintain a neutral-to-positive tone. Criticize the paper not the work.
