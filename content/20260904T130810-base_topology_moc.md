A *base* of a [topology](20260904T104310-topological_space_moc.md) $(X, \tau)$ is a *family* of sets $\mathcal{B}$  which can enumerate any element in $\tau$ through union.

$\displaystyle x = \bigcup_{b \in \mathcal{B}}b, \forall x \in \tau$

and
$\forall x \in B_1 \cap B_2, \exists B_3 \in \mathcal{B} \text{ s.t } x \in B_3 \text{ and } B_3 \subseteq B_1 \cap B_2$

~*For any intersection of two basis, you can find a smaller one that is also a basis*
