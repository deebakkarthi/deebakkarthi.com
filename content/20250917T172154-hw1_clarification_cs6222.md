Problem 2: the notation $max_{T \subseteq U} f(T)$ can be rewritten as $max(\{f(T)\}_{T \subseteq U})$, if that is clearer.
In short, it means for all subsets T of U, compute f(T), and then output the
greatest value among the values of f(T). In this case, $f(T) = |Pr[X \in T] - Pr[Y\in T]|$
